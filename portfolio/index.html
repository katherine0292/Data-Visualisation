    02Audience and traceability受众与可追溯性

    From a worry to a figure从一个顾虑到一张图

    Two personas drive what goes into the story. Every insight is traced to a user story, a figure and a storyboard scene, so each chart has a reason to be there.两个角色决定故事里放什么。每条洞察都能追到用户故事、图表和分镜场景，每张图都有存在的理由。

    Primary persona主要角色

    Mei, 23

    Prospective international student weighing a Sydney Master's.正在考虑赴悉尼读硕士的国际学生。

    trusts official data相信官方数据anecdotes still sway her仍会被传闻影响
    • Wants one dollar figure for her budget, but sources only give an index.想要一个能写进预算的金额，但数据源只给指数。
    • Fears a mistimed application: a A$1,600 visa fee and possibly a lost year.担心申请时点出错：A$1,600 的签证费，还可能赔上一年。
    Secondary persona次要角色

    Daniel, 41

    Education adviser briefing several students at once.同时指导多位学生的教育顾问。

    • Copies charts into client decks, so captions must stand alone.会把图表放进给客户的演示文稿，所以图注必须能独立成立。
    • Avoids quoting a living cost he cannot verify.不会引用自己无法核实的生活成本数字。

    Result: every figure below carries a four-part caption — counts, period, excludes, source.结果：下面每张图都带四段式图注：统计对象、时期、不包含内容、来源。

    10Data and design decisions数据与设计决策

    Four geographies, five datasets四种地理口径，五个数据集

    Each figure uses a different geography and reporting convention. Pick a scope to see which figures use it.每张图使用不同的地理口径和统计惯例。选择一种口径，查看哪些图使用它。

    Design decisions设计决策

    01

    One chart type, one job一图一用

    Trend, composition and geography each get the form that suits the comparison, not one signature chart.趋势、构成、地理各用适合的图形，不套用同一种“招牌图”。

    02

    Log scales for small hubs对数色阶保留小中心

    Values spanning orders of magnitude stay legible; suppressed cells stay blank, not zero.跨越数量级的数值仍然可读；被隐去的单元格留空，而不是 0。

    03

    Sankey replaced换掉桑基图

    It implied a flow between unrelated snapshots. Stacked bars show counts and shares honestly.它暗示了互不相关快照之间的流动。堆叠条形图如实显示数量与占比。

    04

    Budget calculator dropped取消预算计算器

    CPI cannot support a personal dollar figure, so the last scene asks for a buffer instead.CPI 支撑不了个人金额，所以最后一幕改为建议留出缓冲。

    11Limits and evaluation局限与评估

    What the data cannot say, kept visible数据说不了的事，始终放在明处

    Evaluation plan评估计划

    The workbook holds eleven self-assessed criteria and proposed audience checks. The two 80% figures are targets for a future test. No participant testing has been completed, and no result is claimed.工作簿包含十一条自评标准和拟定的受众检验。两个 80% 是未来测试的目标值。尚未完成用户测试，也不声称任何结果。
